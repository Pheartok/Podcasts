## 🎙️ Featured Episode

### [The Hidden Cost of the AI Infrastructure Boom](https://open.spotify.com/episode/3bs9HOlrbw9jl8NTYK2niJ?si=IepSfn7jTAC6Ba6KHvKheQ)

Hundreds of billions of dollars are being poured into building next-generation AI data centers and hyperscale server networks. But while the headline news focuses on models and software capabilities, a much bigger economic story is unfolding behind the scenes: who is paying for all that hardware and power?

**Key Topics Covered:**
* **The Silicon Squeeze:** How major chip manufacturers are shifting production away from standard consumer RAM/DRAM to prioritize high-margin AI server stacks.
* **The Power Grid Crunch:** The massive energy demands of generative AI server farms and regional utility rate hikes.
* **Macro Economics & The Fed:** How central banks view the tension between short-term infrastructure inflation and long-term AI productivity.
